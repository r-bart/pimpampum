# pimpampum

Mini brand brief · exploration 01

## Idea

`pimpampum` should feel highly minimal but never impersonal: a restrained, refined foundation with one memorable visual gesture.

The name has its own rhythm. It can be split into three beats—`pim / pam / pum`—and that sequence can become the center of the identity.

The brand will live primarily on GitHub. It must work especially well on small, practical surfaces: an avatar, README header, social preview, screenshots, and compact diagrams.

## References

The goal is not to copy these references, but to extract their principles:

- [Rauno Freiberg](https://raunofreiberg.com/): personality, clear visual systems, and obsessive attention to detail.
- [Emil Kowalski](https://emilkowal.ski/): functional, precise, restrained motion.
- [Jakub Krehel](https://jakub.kr/): optical precision in typography, radii, shadows, and interaction states.
- [Paco Coursey](https://paco.me/): editorial restraint, generous whitespace, and hierarchy built almost entirely with typography.
- New York and Madrid subway wayfinding: grotesk typography, circular codes, arrows, and color used as information.

## Explored territories

### 1. Three beats

An identity built around `pim · pam · pum`, with three consecutive impacts or states.

This was the initial recommended direction because it turns the sound of the name into a recognizable system and creates room for motion without overloading the brand.

### 2. Editorial terminal

A monochrome, typographic, almost documentary direction. Well suited to GitHub, but at risk of looking like another minimal developer project.

### 3. Soft precision

Warm grays, gentle surfaces, minimal shadows, and one color accent. More tactile and product-oriented.

## Chosen direction: rhythmic wayfinding

The strongest direction combines **Three beats** with the graphic language of subway systems.

`pim`, `pam`, and `pum` behave like three lines, stations, or steps in a journey. Each word can be paired with a numbered circular glyph:

```text
(1) pim
(2) pam
(3) pum →
```

This creates:

- An immediately recognizable visual structure.
- A natural relationship between name, rhythm, and journey.
- A system that can extend to versions, commands, sections, or states.
- Personality without relying on illustration or decoration.

The subway reference should feel like **contemporary wayfinding**, not a literal reproduction of the New York or Madrid identities.

## Initial visual system

### Wordmark

`pimpampum`, always lowercase.

The expressive version may be split into `pim / pam / pum`. The functional version remains joined for repository names, commands, and URLs.

### Typography

A neutral, confident grotesk in the Helvetica and modern-wayfinding tradition.

Initial web stack:

```css
font-family: 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
```

Strength should come from weight, scale, spacing, and composition—not an eccentric typeface.

### Glyphs

- Outlined circles with numbers or letters.
- Simple, heavy arrows.
- Short codes and functional labels.
- Three beats as a recurring graphic unit.

Glyphs must remain legible at very small sizes so they can work as an avatar or icon.

### Initial palette

| Role      | Color     |
| --------- | --------- |
| Paper     | `#F5F3ED` |
| Ink       | `#161614` |
| Red line  | `#EE3F32` |
| Blue line | `#1C57A5` |

Red and blue act as information codes, not decoration. Most compositions should remain ink on paper.

### Motion

Animations follow the rhythm of the name:

1. `pim`
2. `pam`
3. `pum`

Entrances are quick and consecutive; exits are quieter. Motion should be brief, interruptible, and optional—never a constant spectacle.

## Voice

Short, direct sentences with rhythm.

Working examples:

- Three beats. One idea.
- Small outside. Obsessive inside.
- Next stop: something small, made with extraordinary care.
- Local / express / open source.

The tone may be slightly playful, but never childish or excessively clever.

## GitHub application

The README is the primary brand surface and should feel like the project's official website.

Priority assets:

1. Avatar or reduced symbol.
2. README header.
3. Repository social preview.
4. Visual grammar for screenshots, diagrams, and badges.
5. Favicon or avatar for future extensions.

The GitHub user [`@pimpampum`](https://github.com/pimpampum) already exists. This does not prevent using `pimpampum` as a repository name under another account or organization.

## Prototype

The first interactive exploration is available in [`pimpampum.html`](./pimpampum.html).

It includes:

- The `pim / pam / pum` split.
- Three numbered line glyphs.
- Red, blue, ink, and paper palette.
- Wayfinding typography.
- A three-beat microinteraction.
- Adaptation for narrow panels and screens.

## Open questions

Before finalizing the identity, define:

- Exactly what `pimpampum` does.
- Who it is for.
- Whether the brand should feel more technical, cultural, or playful.
- Whether the three colors represent real product concepts.
- Which glyph works best as a standalone symbol.

With those answers, the next step is to create two or three wordmark variants, the avatar, and a real README header.
